How this works

Every item feeds three independent channels: risk (factors that raise concern), neutral (context that neither raises nor lowers concern, counted at 0.3×), and protection (factors that mitigate risk). Protection is capped, never unbounded.

  • Scale answers marked Not assessed are excluded; unanswered items lower coverage.
  • Hard flags force a lower protection cap and are listed explicitly in the result.
  • The net score drives a four-band alert plus an explicit insufficient data band.

This tool produces a structured summary of screening evidence. It is decision support only and must not be used as the sole basis for any decision about a young person.
